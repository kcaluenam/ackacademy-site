Is Your Child On Track?

New York State has specific math skills it expects students to master at each grade level, outlined in the NYS Next Generation Learning Standards. These standards define exactly what your child's teacher is responsible for teaching and what your child is expected to learn in their grade level.

The problem is that most parents never see these standards in plain, understandable english. They only find out their child is behind when a report card arrives or a state test comes back.

Each evaluation is a concise, standards-aligned assessment designed to give you a clear snapshot of your child’s current math readiness, strengths, and gaps relative to grade-level expectations.
