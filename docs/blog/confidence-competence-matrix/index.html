Confidence-Competence Matrix [SMART Worksheets]

Monday December 2, 2024

When your child works on their SMART Worksheets, we use the Confidence-Competence Matrix to understand two key indicators of their learning: their ability to solve problems correctly and their confidence in their solutions. For each problem, students mark how confident they feel about their answer using these simple indicators:

❌ - "I need help with this problem. I'm not sure about my steps or answer."

😬 - "I think I got it right."

👑 - "I'm confident I solved this correctly!"

Understanding what students know and how they feel about each problem helps us support them better. This matrix reveals students' learning states, which shape future SMART Worksheets. For instance, we might find that your child is solving problems correctly but showing low confidence - this tells us they need more encouragement and validation from our mentors as well as more practice with similar types of problems. Or perhaps they indicate high confidence but are getting problems incorrect - this helps us identify where they might have mixed up concepts, like confusing multiplication with addition. This dual tracking helps us understand exactly how your child learns.

The data we gather from this matrix directly influences how we customize your child's future SMART Worksheets and learning path. If we see a student consistently showing low confidence in certain types of problems, we break them down into smaller, more manageable steps. If we notice high confidence and accuracy, we know it's time to introduce the next topic. This step by step approach ensures that your child is always working at the right level, challenged enough to grow but supported enough to succeed.

The Confidence-Competence Matrix is just one of our tools, crucial in helping us deliver the personalized learning experience that A.C.K. Academy strives to provide.
