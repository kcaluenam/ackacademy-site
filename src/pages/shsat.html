SHSAT Prep

The SHSAT is extremely competitive, but preparing for it doesn’t have to be. We’ve created a free, grade-specific guide to help your child stay on track and build the skills they need, with less stress and stronger preparation.

Your free guide includes:

✅ A Skills Evaluation Quiz to check the key concepts your child should have mastered at their grade level

✅ Instant Access to Our Private Video Library with step-by-step solutions to real SHSAT math problems

✅ A Grade-Specific Checklist covering must-know topics, whether they’re starting in 6th grade or reviewing in 8th

✅ A Sample At-Home Worksheet showing how we adapt our curriculum to your child’s unique needs
